DATABASE
--------

The tables are defined in apps/api/prisma/schema/, one file per business area, 40 tables in all.
The same explanations are written, in Indonesian, as comments above each table in those files.
Conventions: most tables also have create_uid, create_date, write_uid and write_date,
which record who created or last changed a row and when. Columns starting with mtpp_
hold rules specific to MTPP.

base.prisma
  Database connection and Prisma setup. Holds no tables.

auth.prisma
  Who can log in and what they may do.
    users
      Login accounts: email, hashed password, active flag, signature. Contact details live in partners.
    groups
      The five roles: Pimpinan, Admin, Sales, Operasional, Finance.
    group_users
      Links each user to their group.
    group_permissions
      Read/create/write/delete rights of each group, per menu.

master.prisma
  Customers, principals, products and prices.
    partners
      All contacts: customers, principals, delivery addresses (children of a customer) and user contacts. Holds the new-customer flag.
    payment_terms
      Payment terms: name and number of days until due.
    sales_pricelists
      Special selling price lists that can be assigned to a customer.
    sales_pricelist_items
      Price list entries: fixed price or discount per product, with minimum quantity and validity dates.
    units
      Product units, such as pcs or metres.
    product_categories
      Product categories.
    product_templates
      Main product data: name, selling price, unit, category, supplying principal, stock or dropship route.
    products
      Product code (SKU) used by transaction lines; points to product_templates.
    supplier_prices
      Principal price list: purchase price and discount per product; the basis of cost and margin.

sales.prisma
  Selling to customers.
    sales_orders
      Quotations (PN) and sales orders (SO) in one table, told apart by state. Holds totals, customer PO, proforma and printed terms.
    sales_order_lines
      Product lines of a quotation or SO: quantity, price, discount, cost, delivered and invoiced quantities.

purchasing.prisma
  Buying from principals.
    purchase_orders
      Purchase orders to principals, created from SOs short of stock; holds payment status and amount due.
    purchase_order_lines
      PO product lines: quantity ordered, received, billed, and purchase price.

stock.prisma
  Warehouses, goods in and out, stock levels.
    warehouses
      Warehouses; each principal has one.
    locations
      Stock locations, inside a warehouse or as a source or destination for goods.
    transfer_types
      Warehouse document types per warehouse: goods receipt or delivery note, with their number code.
    stock_transfers
      Warehouse documents: goods receipts and delivery notes, including backorders.
    stock_moves
      Item lines on a warehouse document; validated ones form the stock movement history.
    stock_balances
      Physical stock balance per product per location.
    stock_rules
      Minimum stock limit per product per warehouse.

accounting.prisma
  Invoices, bills, payments and the books.
    accounts
      Chart of accounts.
    journals
      Journal books, such as sales, purchases, cash and bank.
    journal_entries
      Accounting documents: customer invoices, principal bills, credit notes and journal entries, told apart by move_type.
    journal_entry_lines
      Accounting document lines: billed products and debit and credit per account.
    payments
      Money received from customers, including advances, and paid to principals.
    bank_statement_lines
      Bank statement lines imported from CSV, for reconciling with payments.

system.prisma
  Shared features used across the app.
    messages
      Internal notes and history entries on any document.
    change_logs
      Old and new value of each changed field, tied to one messages entry.
    email_logs
      A record of every outgoing email and its delivery status.
    attachments
      Attachment and signature file records; the files themselves are in the storage volume.
    activities
      Scheduled activities on documents, shown under the bell icon.
    settings
      System settings as key and value pairs: company, tax, document terms.
    document_sequences
      Document number counters per code, year and month.
    saved_filters
      Favourite filters saved by each user.
    api_keys
      REST API keys for other applications; only the hash is stored.
    documents
      Official letters, handover and commissioning reports, maintenance reports; they don't affect stock or journals.

apps/api/prisma/migrations/
  The history of database changes, applied in date order when the API starts. Never edit an old one.
    0_init/migration.sql
      Creates all the original tables
    20261105090000_po_note_ppn/migration.sql
      Updates the printed PO note about tax
    20261105100000_doc_notes_no_returns/migration.sql
      Updates printed PO and invoice notes about returns
    20261106090000_quotation_number/migration.sql
      Keeps the PN number when a quotation becomes an SO
    20261106100000_quotation_terms/migration.sql
      Adds the handover and standard terms
    20261106120000_indexes_relations_state_rules/migration.sql
      Adds indexes, 5 table links and 7 state rules
    migration_lock.toml
      Records that the database is PostgreSQL

How the main tables connect to an order:

  sales_orders ------> sales_order_lines
     |   |
     |   +--------> stock_transfers (delivery note) ---> stock_moves ---> stock_balances
     |
     +------------> purchase_orders ---> purchase_order_lines
     |                   |
     |                   +--> stock_transfers (goods receipt) ---> stock_moves
     |                   +--> journal_entries (principal bill) ---> payments
     |
     +------------> journal_entries (customer invoice) ---> payments
                         |
                         +--> journal_entry_lines
