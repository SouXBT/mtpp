MTPP DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM - HOW ONE ORDER MOVES THROUGH EACH ROLE
=================================================================

Open the app at http://localhost:8080
Button and menu names are written exactly as they appear on screen, in [brackets].
"Menu > Sub-menu" means click the menu in the top bar, then the sub-menu under it.
The web address is shown in (brackets) in case you want to go there directly.


THE SHORT VERSION
-----------------

  SALES        1. Make the quotation          Penjualan > Penawaran
               2. Send it to the customer
               3. Customer agrees: Konfirmasi (quotation PN/... becomes sales order SO/...)
                                  |
          +-----------------------+-----------------------+
          | Stock too low         | New customer          | Stock available
          v                       v                       |
  FINANCE 4. Make + confirm       Record the customer's   |
             PO to the principal  payment (must be paid   |
          |                       in full before sending) |
          v                       |                       |
  OPERASIONAL 5. Receive goods    |                       |
          |                       |                       |
          v                       |                       |
  FINANCE 6. Record + pay the     |                       |
             principal's bill     |                       |
          |                       |                       |
          +-----------------------+-----------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
  OPERASIONAL  7. Make + validate the delivery note       Stok > Siap Dikirim
                                  |
                                  v
  FINANCE      8. Make + post the invoice                 Penagihan > Siap Ditagih
               9. Record the customer's payment           [Catat Pembayaran]
                                  |
                                  v
                                 DONE

  One order can take more than one path at once. For example, part of it can ship
  from stock now while the rest waits for a PO.

  TIP: Open the sales order at any time (Penjualan > Sales Order, click the row).
  A note at the top starting "Berikutnya:" tells you who needs to do what next.


WHO DOES WHAT
-------------

  Role          Login                     Works in
  ------------  ------------------------  ------------------------------------------------
  Sales         sales@mtpp.co.id          Penjualan (quotations and sales orders)
  Finance       finance@mtpp.co.id        Pengadaan, Penagihan, Akuntansi
  Operasional   operasional@mtpp.co.id    Stok (receiving goods, delivery notes)
  Admin         admin@mtpp.co.id          Master data, Pengaturan. Cannot open orders.
  Pimpinan      pimpinan@mtpp.co.id       Sees everything. Sets payment terms and selling
                                          price lists. Cannot create orders.

  Demo password for all of them: mtpp12345

  To log in:  /login -> type Email and Kata sandi -> [Masuk] -> you land on Beranda.
  To log out: click your name at the top right -> Keluar.


BEFORE THE FIRST ORDER (set up once)
------------------------------------

  ADMIN
    New customer:   Master > Pelanggan (/master/pelanggan), add the customer.
                    New customers have "Pelanggan baru (wajib lunas sebelum kirim)" ticked
                    automatically. Leave it ticked if they must pay before delivery.
    New principal:  Master > Principal (/master/principal). A warehouse is created for it
                    automatically.
    New product:    Master > Produk (/master/produk). Choose its principal.

  FINANCE
    Purchase price: Pengadaan > Pricelist Principal (/pengadaan/pricelist).
                    Without a price here, the PO in Step 4 is refused.

  PIMPINAN (optional)
    Payment terms:  Master > Term of Payment (/master/top)
    Special prices: Master > Pricelist Penjualan (/master/pricelist)


=================================================================
EXAMPLE 1: REGULAR CUSTOMER, NOT ENOUGH STOCK
The customer orders 500 pieces. Only 40 are in stock, so 460 must be ordered.
This is the longest path, and it uses all of the main steps.
=================================================================

STEP 1 - SALES: make the quotation
  Go to:    Penjualan > Penawaran (/penjualan?tab=quote)
  Click:    [Penawaran baru]
  You are:  on the new quotation page (/penjualan/baru)
  Fill in:  Pelanggan        choose the customer (required)
            Term of payment  filled from the customer, can be changed
            Berlaku sampai   the date the quotation expires
            PO Pelanggan     customer's PO number, if you have it already
            Alamat kirim     delivery address
            Rencana kirim    planned delivery date
  Then:     on the "Baris Produk" tab, click [Tambah baris]
            Produk           choose the product
            Qty              type 500
            Harga (Rp)       filled automatically, can be changed
            Diskon           optional
            Repeat [Tambah baris] for each product.
  Optional: the "Syarat Penawaran" tab holds the printed terms (Pembayaran, Penyerahan,
            Standar, Waktu kirim, Masa berlaku, Garansi).
  Click:    [Simpan]
  Result:   the quotation gets a number like PN/MTPP/2026/11/0001.
            You stay on its page (/penjualan/1).

STEP 2 - SALES: send it to the customer
  Where:    same page
  Click:    [Kirim Email] to email it, or [Cetak] to get the PDF.
            [Kirim Email] stays greyed out until a product has a price.
  Result:   status becomes Terkirim. If no Brevo email key is set, the email is
            only simulated and nothing is really sent.

STEP 3 - SALES: the customer agrees
  Where:    same page. To come back later: Penjualan > Penawaran, click the row.
  Click:    [Konfirmasi]
  Dialog:   "Konfirmasi menjadi sales order?" -> type the PO Pelanggan -> [Konfirmasi]
  Result:   the number changes to SO/MTPP/2026/11/0001 and product lines are locked.
  Screen:   "Berikutnya: Finance membuat PO Principal."
            "Berikutnya: Operasional membuat surat jalan."  (for the 40 in stock)
  Sales is finished for now, and can follow progress on this page.

STEP 4 - FINANCE: order the missing goods from the principal
  Go to:    Pengadaan > Perlu PO Principal (/pengadaan/perlu-pesanan)
  See:      every sales order that is short of stock
  Click:    [Buat PO] on the order's row
            (or open the sales order and click [Buat PO Principal])
  You are:  on the new PO page (/pengadaan/1), number PO/MTPP/2026/11/0001, quantity 460.
            If the products come from 2 principals, you get 2 POs and stay on the list.
  Click:    [Kirim Email] to send it to the principal, or [Cetak] for the PDF
            (email needs the principal's email address to be filled in).
  Click:    [Konfirmasi] -> dialog "Konfirmasi PO?" -> [Konfirmasi]
  Result:   a goods receipt is created automatically for Operasional.

STEP 5 - OPERASIONAL: goods arrive from the principal
  Go to:    Stok > Penerimaan Barang (/stok/penerimaan)
  Click:    the row of the PO (the whole row is clickable)
  You are:  on the receipt page (/stok/dok/1), number PO/MTPP/2026/11/0001-T1
  Check:    "Diterima" is already filled with the ordered quantity (460).
            Change it if fewer arrived.
  Click:    [Validasi] -> dialog "Validasi penerimaan barang?" -> [Validasi]
  Result:   physical stock goes up. Optional: [Cetak bukti penerimaan] prints the proof.
  If fewer arrived: the dialog warns you, and a follow-up receipt is created for the rest.

STEP 6 - FINANCE: pay the principal
  Go to:    Pengadaan > PO Principal (/pengadaan), click the PO
  Click:    [Catat Tagihan]
  Dialog:   Nomor tagihan dari principal, Tanggal tagihan, Lama pembayaran,
            File tagihan principal -> [Simpan tagihan]
  You are:  on the bill page (/pengadaan/tagihan/1), number BILL/MTPP/2026/11/0001
  Click:    [Posting]  (the journal entry is created)
  Click:    [Catat Pembayaran]
  Dialog:   Jumlah (Rp), Metode (Transfer bank or Kas), Tanggal, Keterangan
            -> [Catat pembayaran]
  Result:   the bill is Lunas. To see all bills: Pengadaan > Tagihan Principal.
  Note:     paying the principal does not have to come before delivery. Step 7 only
            needs the goods to be in stock.

STEP 7 - OPERASIONAL: deliver to the customer
  Go to:    Stok > Siap Dikirim (/stok/siap-dikirim)
  Click:    [Buat Surat Jalan] on the order's row
  You are:  on the delivery note page (/stok/dok/2), number like DO/MTPP/2026/11/0001
  Check:    "Dikirim" is the quantity going on the truck. Lower it to ship in parts.
  Click:    [Cetak surat jalan (3 lembar)] for the 3-copy paper delivery note
  Click:    [Siap Kirim] (optional: marks it as ready to go)
  Click:    [Validasi] -> dialog "Validasi surat jalan?" -> [Validasi]
  Result:   physical stock goes down.
  Screen:   the sales order now says "Berikutnya: Finance membuat invoice."
  If you shipped less: a follow-up delivery note is created for the rest.

STEP 8 - FINANCE: invoice the customer
  Go to:    Penagihan > Siap Ditagih (/penagihan/siap)
  Click:    [Buat Invoice] on the order's row
  You are:  on the draft invoice page (/penagihan/3)
  Check:    the lines, then click [Posting]
  Result:   number like INV/MTPP/2026/11/0001, journal entry created.
  Click:    [Kirim Email] to send it, or [Cetak] for the PDF
            (email needs the customer's email address to be filled in).

STEP 9 - FINANCE: the customer pays
  Where:    same invoice page. To come back later: Penagihan > Invoice, click the row.
  Click:    [Catat Pembayaran]
  Dialog:   Jumlah (Rp), Metode (Transfer bank or Kas), Tanggal, Keterangan
            -> [Catat pembayaran]
  Result:   the invoice shows Lunas if paid in full, or Sebagian if partly paid.
  Receipt:  Penagihan > Pembayaran (/penagihan/pembayaran) -> [Kuitansi] on the row.

  THE ORDER IS FINISHED.


=================================================================
EXAMPLE 2: NEW CUSTOMER, STOCK AVAILABLE
A new customer must pay in full before anything can be delivered.
=================================================================

STEPS 1-3 - SALES: same as Example 1.
  Screen after Konfirmasi:
    "Pelanggan baru: dibayar Rp 0 dari Rp 516.150. Surat jalan diterbitkan setelah lunas."
    "Berikutnya: Finance mencatat pembayaran pelanggan."

  While unpaid:
    - The order does NOT appear in Stok > Siap Dikirim.
    - On the sales order page, Operasional sees [Buat Surat Jalan] greyed out.

STEP 4 - FINANCE: ask for payment (optional)
  Go to:    Penjualan > Sales Order (/penjualan?tab=order), click the order
  Click:    [Proforma Invoice]
  Dialog:   choose Berdasarkan persen or Berdasarkan nominal, then Tanggal and
            Catatan pembayaran -> [Terbitkan dan cetak] or [Terbitkan dan kirim email]
  Result:   a proforma invoice number (PI). It creates no journal entry.

STEP 5 - FINANCE: record the payment
  Where:    same sales order page
  Click:    [Catat Uang Muka / Pelunasan]
  Dialog:   Jumlah (Rp), Metode, Tanggal, Keterangan -> [Catat pembayaran]
  Result:   "Pelanggan baru: sales order sudah lunas, surat jalan dapat diterbitkan."
  Partial:  record a smaller amount and the delivery stays blocked until the rest is paid.

STEP 6 - OPERASIONAL: deliver
  Same as Step 7 in Example 1. The order now appears in Stok > Siap Dikirim.

STEP 7 - FINANCE: invoice
  Same as Step 8 in Example 1.
  The payment from Step 5 is applied automatically when the invoice is posted, so there
  is nothing more to collect unless the order changed.


=================================================================
EXAMPLE 3: DROPSHIP
The principal sends the goods straight to the customer. MTPP's stock does not change.
=================================================================

STEPS 1-3 - SALES: same as Example 1, using a dropship product.
  Screen: "Berikutnya: Finance membuat PO Principal."

STEP 4 - FINANCE: order from the principal
  Go to:    Pengadaan > Perlu PO Principal -> [Buat PO]
  Click:    [Konfirmasi] -> dialog says "Surat jalan dropship dibuat otomatis." -> [Konfirmasi]
  Result:   a delivery note is created automatically.

STEP 5 - OPERASIONAL: confirm the customer received it
  Go to:    Stok > Surat Jalan (/stok/surat-jalan), click the row marked "dropship"
  Click:    [Validasi] -> "Konfirmasi barang dropship diterima pelanggan?" -> [Validasi]
  Result:   stock does not change.
  Screen:   "Berikutnya: Finance membuat invoice."

STEP 6 - FINANCE: invoice and payment
  Same as Steps 8 and 9 in Example 1. Pay the principal as in Step 6 of Example 1.


=================================================================
WHEN SOMETHING CHANGES
=================================================================

  Cancel a quotation or order (Sales)
    On its page, click the [...] button at the right -> Batalkan.
    To reuse it: [Kembalikan ke Draf]. To copy it: [...] -> Salin penawaran.
    An order cannot be cancelled while its PO is running or a customer advance is
    still held.

  Give back an advance (Finance)
    On the sales order page: [Kembalikan Uang Muka].

  Principal will never send the rest (Finance)
    On the PO page: [Tutup sisa PO]. The PO shrinks to what arrived, and the rest can be
    ordered again from the sales order.

  Cancel a PO (Finance)
    On the PO page: [Batalkan]. Not possible once goods have been partly received.

  Credit note from a principal (Finance)
    On the PO page: [Nota Kredit].

  Undo a customer or principal payment (Finance)
    Penagihan > Pembayaran -> Batalkan Pembayaran on the row.

  Undo a posted invoice or bill (Finance)
    On its page: [Batalkan (jurnal pembalik)]. Posted journals are never edited, a
    reversing entry is created instead.

  Quotation expired
    It is marked Kedaluwarsa. It can still be confirmed, but the dialog warns you.


=================================================================
PAGES EACH ROLE WATCHES
=================================================================

  SALES         Penjualan > Penawaran       quotations waiting for the customer
                Penjualan > Sales Order     confirmed orders and their progress
  FINANCE       Pengadaan > Perlu PO Principal   orders needing a PO
                Pengadaan > Tagihan Principal    bills to post and pay
                Penagihan > Siap Ditagih         delivered, not yet invoiced
                Penagihan > Invoice              invoices and who still owes money
                Akuntansi > Kas, Jurnal, Buku Besar, Neraca Saldo, Rekonsiliasi Bank
  OPERASIONAL   Stok > Penerimaan Barang    goods expected from principals
                Stok > Siap Dikirim         orders that can ship now
                Stok > Surat Jalan          delivery schedule, late ones marked
                Stok > Stok per Gudang      physical, reserved and free stock
                Stok > Mutasi               history of every stock movement
  PIMPINAN      Beranda and Laporan         the numbers across the whole business
  ADMIN         Master, Pengaturan          customers, products, users, company data
  EVERYONE      The bell at the top         scheduled activities
