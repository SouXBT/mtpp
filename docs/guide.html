MTPP distribution system

System guide

How one order moves through each role, and where everything lives in the project folder.

Order flowProject sitemap

From quotation to payment

Button names appear exactly as on screen. Open the app at http://localhost:8080.

At any time, open the sales order at Penjualan > Sales Order. A note starting with “Berikutnya” says who needs to act next.

Who does what

RoleLoginWorks in
Salessales@mtpp.co.idPenjualan: quotations and sales orders
Financefinance@mtpp.co.idPengadaan, Penagihan, Akuntansi
Operasionaloperasional@mtpp.co.idStok: receiving goods and delivery notes
Adminadmin@mtpp.co.idMaster data and Pengaturan. Can’t open orders.
Pimpinanpimpinan@mtpp.co.idSees everything, sets payment terms and selling prices. Can’t create orders.

Example 1: regular customer, not enough stock

The customer orders 500 pieces and only 40 are in stock, so 460 must be ordered. This path uses every main step.

  1. Quotation
  2. Konfirmasi
  3. PO
  4. Receive
  5. Pay bill
  6. Delivery note
  7. Invoice
  8. Payment
  1. 1

    Make the quotation Sales

    Go to
    Penjualan > Penawaran, then Penawaran baru
    Fill in
    • Pelanggan (required), Term of payment, Berlaku sampai, PO Pelanggan, Alamat kirim, Rencana kirim
    • On the Baris Produk tab, click Tambah baris for each product: Produk, Qty 500. Harga fills in by itself and can be changed
    • Berlaku sampai must be after the quotation date; it can’t be the same day
    • The printed terms are on the Syarat Penawaran tab
    Then
    Simpan
    Result
    A number like PN/MTPP/2026/11/0001. You stay on the quotation page.
  2. 2

    Send it to the customer Sales

    Then
    Kirim Email, or Cetak for the PDF. Kirim Email stays grey until a product has a price.
    Result
    Status becomes Terkirim. Without a Brevo email key, the email is only simulated.
  3. 3

    Customer agrees Sales

    Then
    Konfirmasi, type the PO Pelanggan in the dialog, then Konfirmasi
    Result
    The number changes to SO/MTPP/2026/11/0001 and product lines lock.
    Screen says
    “Berikutnya: Finance membuat PO Principal” and “Berikutnya: Operasional membuat surat jalan” for the 40 in stock.
  4. 4

    Order the missing goods Finance

    Go to
    Pengadaan > Perlu PO Principal, then Buat PO on the row
    You land on
    The new PO, PO/MTPP/2026/11/0001, for 460 pieces. Products from 2 principals make 2 POs, and you stay on the list.
    Then
    Kirim Email or Cetak, then Konfirmasi and Konfirmasi in the dialog
    Result
    A goods receipt is created for Operasional.
  5. 5

    Goods arrive from the principal Operasional

    Go to
    Stok > Penerimaan Barang, then click the PO’s row
    Check
    “Diterima” already shows 460. Lower it if fewer arrived, and a follow-up receipt is made for the rest.
    Then
    Validasi, then Validasi in the dialog
    Result
    Physical stock goes up. Cetak bukti penerimaan prints the proof.
  6. 6

    Record and pay the principal’s bill Finance

    Go to
    Pengadaan > PO Principal, then click the PO
    Then
    Catat Tagihan, fill in Nomor tagihan, Tanggal, Lama pembayaran, File, then Simpan tagihan
    Next page
    BILL/MTPP/2026/11/0001: click Posting, then Catat Pembayaran, fill in the amount and method, then Catat pembayaran
    Result
    The bill is Lunas. Delivery doesn’t wait for this step, only for the goods.
  7. 7

    Deliver to the customer Operasional

    Go to
    Stok > Siap Dikirim, then Buat Surat Jalan on the row
    Check
    “Dikirim” is what goes on the truck. Lower it to ship in parts, and a follow-up note is made for the rest.
    Then
    Cetak surat jalan (3 lembar), optionally Siap Kirim, then Validasi and Validasi in the dialog
    Result
    Physical stock goes down.
    Screen says
    “Berikutnya: Finance membuat invoice.”
  8. 8

    Invoice the customer Finance

    Go to
    Penagihan > Siap Ditagih, then Buat Invoice on the row
    Then
    Check the lines, click Posting, then Kirim Email or Cetak
    Result
    A number like INV/MTPP/2026/11/0001, and the journal entry is created.
  9. 9

    Customer pays Finance

    Then
    On the invoice, Catat Pembayaran, fill in the amount and method, then Catat pembayaran
    Result
    The invoice shows Lunas, or Sebagian if partly paid. Print the receipt from Penagihan > Pembayaran with Kuitansi.

Example 2: new customer, stock available

A new customer must pay in full before anything ships.

  1. Quotation
  2. Konfirmasi
  3. Proforma
  4. Payment
  5. Delivery note
  6. Invoice
  1. 1–3

    Quotation to sales order Sales

    Do
    The same as steps 1 to 3 above.
    Screen says
    “Pelanggan baru: dibayar Rp 0 dari Rp 516.150. Surat jalan diterbitkan setelah lunas.”
    While unpaid
    The order isn’t listed in Siap Dikirim, and Operasional sees Buat Surat Jalan greyed out on the order page.
  2. 4

    Ask for payment (optional) Finance

    Go to
    Penjualan > Sales Order, then click the order
    Then
    Proforma Invoice, choose Uang muka (persen), Uang muka (nominal) or Langsung lunas. For a down payment, fill in Sisa dilunasi paling lambat. Then Terbitkan dan cetak or Terbitkan dan kirim email
    Result
    A proforma number, printed with the payoff date. No journal entry is created.
  3. 5

    Record the payment Finance

    Then
    On the same page, Catat Pembayaran (DP / Lunas). Choose Uang muka (DP) with the amount and the payoff date, or Lunasi langsung for the whole remaining amount. Attach the transfer proof (image or PDF), then Catat pembayaran
    Rules
    Without a proof the button stays grey. A down payment needs a payoff date; the payoff must equal what is still owed.
    Result
    “Pelanggan baru: sales order sudah lunas, surat jalan dapat diterbitkan.” After a down payment the message shows the remaining amount and the payoff date, in red once it has passed. Every payment is listed in Penjualan > Riwayat Pembayaran as Uang muka ke-n, Pelunasan or Lunas langsung, with the proof and a receipt.
  4. 6

    Deliver Operasional

    Do
    The same as step 7 above. The order now appears in Stok > Siap Dikirim.
  5. 7

    Invoice Finance

    Do
    The same as step 8 above. The payment from step 5 is applied automatically when you click Posting.

Example 4: regular customer pays in installments

Only for regular customers. New customers still pay in full before delivery.

  1. Atur Cicilan
  2. Setujui Jadwal
  3. Payment per installment
  4. Reminder
  1. 1

    Propose the schedule Finance

    Go to
    Penagihan > Invoice, then click the posted invoice
    Then
    Atur Cicilan: set Termin, Mulai and Setiap … hari, click Bagi Rata, adjust dates or amounts, add the agreement note, then Ajukan Jadwal
    Rules
    At least 2 and at most 12 installments, dates in order, the last one within 12 months of the invoice date, and the total equal to the amount still owed
    Result
    The Cicilan tab shows “Menunggu persetujuan”. Until it is approved, the invoice due date still applies.
  2. 2

    Approve the schedule Finance

    Who
    Any user allowed to change billing data (write access on Penagihan)
    Then
    On the Cicilan tab, Setujui Jadwal
    Result
    The due date shown becomes the oldest unpaid installment. The printed invoice lists the schedule.
  3. 3

    Record each payment Finance

    Then
    Catat Pembayaran as usual
    Result
    Money goes to the oldest installment first. Each row shows Lunas, Sebagian or Belum. The aging report shows each open installment on its own row.
  4. 4

    Remind the customer Finance

    Then
    Kirim Pengingat emails the next installment, its amount and due date, with the invoice PDF
    Change the plan
    Ubah Cicilan proposes a new schedule for what is still owed, which must be approved again. Batalkan Jadwal goes back to the invoice due date.

Late payment: grace period and delivery hold

Each customer has a grace period after the due date. Pimpinan sets it per customer; when it is empty, the default in Pengaturan > Format Cetak > Penagihan & Retur (7 days) is used.

  1. Grace period per customer
  2. Due date
  3. Masa tenggang
  4. Delivery held
  5. Izinkan Kirim
  1. 1

    Set the grace period Pimpinan

    Go to
    Master > Pelanggan, then the customer
    Fill in
    Masa tenggang pembayaran (hari), 0 to 90
    Then
    Simpan
  2. 2

    Watch the status Finance

    Go to
    Penagihan > Invoice, filter Status penagihan
    Shows
    Belum jatuh tempo, then a yellow “Masa tenggang s.d. …” tag after the due date, then a red “Lewat masa tenggang” tag. Use Kirim Pengingat during the grace period.
    Note
    The aging report still counts days from the due date. The grace period only decides when deliveries stop.
  3. 3

    New order for that customer Sales

    Then
    Konfirmasi still works; the dialog warns about the overdue invoices
    Screen says
    “Tagihan lewat masa tenggang: INV/…” and Buat Surat Jalan is greyed out. The order isn’t delivered until the invoice is paid.
  4. 4

    Allow delivery anyway Pimpinan

    Go to
    The sales order
    Then
    Izinkan Kirim on the red message, type the reason, then Izinkan Kirim
    Result
    Operasional can deliver this one order. Cabut Izin takes it back. Agreeing to installments also lifts the hold once the schedule is approved.

Example 5: goods arrive with a problem

The customer reports damaged, wrong or out-of-spec goods from a delivery note that is already done. Claims should come within 7 days of delivery; later claims are allowed but marked late.

  1. Laporkan Barang Bermasalah
  2. Replacement
  3. Credit note or discount
  4. Refund
  1. 1

    Report the problem Operasional or Sales

    Go to
    Stok > Surat Jalan, then the finished delivery note
    Then
    Laporkan Barang Bermasalah: choose Alasan, then the Penyelesaian, fill in Qty bermasalah per product and a note, then Buat Retur
    Choose
    Kirim pengganti: goods come back and a replacement is sent. Retur dan nota kredit: goods come back and the customer owes less. Potongan harga: the customer keeps the goods for an agreed discount per unit.
    Result
    A draft RET/MTPP/2026/11/0001. Print the Cetak Berita Acara for the customer to sign.
  2. 2a

    Replacement Operasional

    Then
    On the return, Proses Retur
    Result
    The goods go to the warehouse’s Barang Rusak location, not to free stock. A replacement delivery note is created at once and marked Pengganti retur in lists, on the order and on the print. The order shows the quantity in a Diretur column. Validate it as in step 7 of Example 1; if there isn’t enough stock, Finance orders it from Pengadaan > Perlu PO Principal. No new invoice is made.
  3. 2b

    Credit note or discount Finance

    Then
    On the return, Proses Retur
    Result
    A credit note (Retur Penjualan and PPN Keluaran against Piutang) lowers what is still owed on the order’s invoice. Only goods that were already invoiced can be credited. For a return, the goods go to Barang Rusak.
    Already paid
    If the invoice was already paid, the credit stays with the customer: Kembalikan Dana pays it back from bank or cash.

Example 6: send damaged goods back to the principal

Goods in a warehouse’s Barang Rusak location (from customer returns) go back to the principal they were bought from.

  1. Retur ke Principal
  2. Replacement arrives
  3. Credit note
  1. 1

    Make the return Finance or Operasional

    Go to
    Pengadaan > PO Principal, the PO the goods came from, then Retur ke Principal
    Fill in
    Choose Principal mengirim barang pengganti or Nota kredit dari principal, then Qty dikembalikan per product (at most what is in Barang Rusak), then Buat Retur
    Result
    A draft RP/MTPP/2026/11/0001 in Pengadaan > Retur ke Principal. Cetak Bukti Retur prints the slip that goes with the goods.
  2. 2a

    Replacement from the principal Operasional

    Then
    On the return, Proses Retur
    Result
    The goods leave Barang Rusak and a goods receipt for the replacement is created. When it arrives, validate it in Stok > Penerimaan Barang; physical stock goes up. The PO’s received quantity and bills don’t change.
  3. 2b

    Credit note from the principal Finance

    Then
    On the return, Proses Retur
    Result
    The goods leave Barang Rusak and a credit note (Utang Usaha against Pembelian and PPN Masukan) lowers what MTPP still owes on the PO. Only goods the principal has already billed can be credited.

When something changes

SituationRoleWhat to do
Cancel a quotation or orderSalesOn its page, the ... button, then Batalkan. Kembalikan ke Draf reuses it. Not possible while its PO is running. An unused advance is not given back: it is forfeited (Uang muka hangus, booked as other income) and the order can no longer go back to draft; use Salin penawaran instead.
Copy a quotationSalesThe ... button, then Salin penawaran.
Principal will never send the restFinanceOn the PO, Tutup sisa PO. The PO shrinks to what arrived, and the rest can be ordered again.
Cancel a POFinanceOn the PO, Batalkan. Not possible once goods are partly received.
Credit note from a principalFinanceOn the PO, Nota Kredit.
Wrong quantity on a draft returnOperasionalOn the return, Ubah or Batalkan. A processed return can’t be undone; correct it with a journal adjustment.
Dropship goods are faultyFinanceChoose credit note or discount; replacement from MTPP’s warehouse isn’t possible. The goods go back to the principal directly.
Undo a paymentFinancePenagihan > Pembayaran, then Batalkan Pembayaran on the row.
Undo a posted invoice or billFinanceOn its page, Batalkan (jurnal pembalik). Posted journals aren’t edited; a reversing entry is made.

Database

45 tables, one schema file per business area, in apps/api/prisma/schema/. The same explanations are written in Indonesian above each table in those files. Most tables also record who created or last changed a row and when. Columns starting with mtpp_ hold rules specific to MTPP.

auth.prisma

Who can log in and what they may do.

users
Login accounts: email, hashed password, active flag, signature. Contact details live in partners.
groups
The five roles: Pimpinan, Admin, Sales, Operasional, Finance.
group_users
Links each user to their group.
group_permissions
Read/create/write/delete rights of each group, per menu.

master.prisma

Customers, principals, products and prices.

partners
All contacts: customers, principals, delivery addresses (children of a customer) and user contacts. Holds the new-customer flag and the payment grace period.
payment_terms
Payment terms: name and number of days until due.
sales_pricelists
Special selling price lists that can be assigned to a customer.
sales_pricelist_items
Price list entries: fixed price or discount per product, with minimum quantity and validity dates.
units
Product units, such as pcs or metres.
product_categories
Product categories.
product_templates
Main product data: name, selling price, unit, category, supplying principal, stock or dropship route.
products
Product code (SKU) used by transaction lines; points to product_templates.
supplier_prices
Principal price list: purchase price and discount per product; the basis of cost and margin.

sales.prisma

Selling to customers.

sales_orders
Quotations (PN) and sales orders (SO) in one table, told apart by state. Holds totals, customer PO, proforma and printed terms.
sales_order_lines
Product lines of a quotation or SO: quantity, price, discount, cost, delivered and invoiced quantities.
sales_returns
Customer returns and complaints from a finished delivery note: reason, resolution (replacement, credit note, discount) and the credit note made.
sales_return_lines
Items on a return: the delivery line, quantity, and price or discount per unit.

purchasing.prisma

Buying from principals.

purchase_orders
Purchase orders to principals, created from SOs short of stock; holds payment status and amount due.
purchase_order_lines
PO product lines: quantity ordered, received, billed, and purchase price.
purchase_returns
Damaged goods sent back to a principal for one PO: replacement or credit note, and the receipt or credit note made.
purchase_return_lines
Items on a return to a principal: PO line, quantity and purchase price.

stock.prisma

Warehouses, goods in and out, stock levels.

warehouses
Warehouses; each principal has one.
locations
Stock locations, inside a warehouse or as a source or destination for goods.
transfer_types
Warehouse document types per warehouse: goods receipt or delivery note, with their number code.
stock_transfers
Warehouse documents: goods receipts and delivery notes, including backorders.
stock_moves
Item lines on a warehouse document; validated ones form the stock movement history.
stock_balances
Physical stock balance per product per location.
stock_rules
Minimum stock limit per product per warehouse.

accounting.prisma

Invoices, bills, payments and the books.

accounts
Chart of accounts.
journals
Journal books, such as sales, purchases, cash and bank.
journal_entries
Accounting documents: customer invoices, principal bills, credit notes and journal entries, told apart by move_type.
journal_entry_lines
Accounting document lines: billed products and debit and credit per account.
invoice_installments
Installment schedule of a customer invoice: due date and amount per installment, and who approved it.
payments
Money received from customers, including advances, and paid to principals.
bank_statement_lines
Bank statement lines imported from CSV, for reconciling with payments.

system.prisma

Shared features used across the app.

messages
Internal notes and history entries on any document.
change_logs
Old and new value of each changed field, tied to one messages entry.
email_logs
A record of every outgoing email and its delivery status.
attachments
Attachment and signature file records; the files themselves are in the storage volume.
activities
Scheduled activities on documents, shown under the bell icon.
settings
System settings as key and value pairs: company, tax, document terms.
document_sequences
Document number counters per code, year and month.
saved_filters
Favourite filters saved by each user.
api_keys
REST API keys for other applications; only the hash is stored.
documents
Official letters, handover and commissioning reports, maintenance reports; they don't affect stock or journals.
