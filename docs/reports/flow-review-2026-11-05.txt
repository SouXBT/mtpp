MTPP DISTRIBUSI - FLOW REVIEW
Date: 5 November 2026
Project: ~/Downloads/mtpp


SUMMARY
-------
The normal flow works. scripts/demo-flow.mjs ran from start to finish and every check
passed. The problems appear once something goes off the normal path: a cancellation, a
reversal, a short delivery, or two orders competing for the same stock.

This review found 22 points:
  - A: 11 points where the software gives a wrong or inconsistent result. Fix these first.
  - B: 6 points where the software works, but a business rule needs your decision.
  - C: 5 points where the screens or wording are confusing.

Nothing was fixed. This review changed no source code.

Start with A1, A2, A3 and A4. They affect stock and money, and they can come up in a
normal demo.


HOW THIS WAS CHECKED
--------------------

[UI]      Done on the real screen in the temporary copy.
[CODE]    Read from the source code, not run.

1. HOW THE FLOW WORKS
---------------------
Step  Who           Action                        Result
1     Sales         Create quotation              Status Draf, numbered SO/MTPP/...
2     Sales         Send email (optional)         Status Terkirim
3     Sales         Konfirmasi                    Status Sales Order, lines locked
4     Finance       Buat Pesanan Principal        Draft PO, one per principal, only for the
                                                  shortage. Dropship goes on a separate PO.
5     Finance       Confirm PO                    Creates a receipt PO/...-T1, or a dropship
                                                  delivery order numbered DO/...
6     Operasional   Validate receipt              Stock increases. Any remainder goes to -T2.
7     Finance       New customers only: proforma  Proforma makes no journal entry. Advance
                    and advance payment           goes to Uang Muka Pelanggan.
8     Operasional   Buat Surat Jalan              New customers are blocked until fully paid.
9     Operasional   Siap Kirim, then Validasi     Stock decreases. Remainder goes to a new DO.
10    Finance       Buat Invoice, then Posting    Bills delivered quantity only. Advance is
                                                  applied automatically.
11    Finance       Record customer payment       Journal BNK or KAS, receipt (kuitansi)
12    Finance       Record principal bill, post,  Bill covers received quantity only. PO
                    then pay                      payment status updates.
13    System        SO becomes Selesai            When fully delivered and fully invoiced
14    Finance       Cash, journals, ledger,       Separate from the document flow
                    trial balance, bank
                    reconciliation

Dropship: steps 4 and 5 create the delivery order directly. There is no Buat Surat Jalan
step, and warehouse stock does not change.


2. A - WRONG OR INCONSISTENT RESULTS
------------------------------------

A1. One order can take stock already reserved for another order  [TESTED]  HIGH
    Test:   Stock was 2. SO-A ordered 2 and showed no shortage. SO-B ordered 2 and showed
            a shortage of 2, but its delivery order was still allowed and validated.
    Result: Free stock became -2, and SO-A now shows a shortage of 2.
    Why:    The delivery checks compare against physical stock, not free stock.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/stock/transfers.service.ts:156 and :175
    Decide: Should a delivery be limited to free stock, or can Operasional knowingly
            override it?

A2. Reversing an unpaid principal bill marks the PO as "lunas"  [TESTED]  HIGH
    Test:   Posted a bill, paid nothing, then reversed it.
    Result: PO status "lunas", with 0 billed and goods still received.
    Why:    A reversed bill counts the same as a paid one. It should go back to
            "menunggu tagihan".
    Where:  apps/api/src/common/status.ts:32

A3. Reversing a payment journal leaves the invoice "Lunas"  [TESTED]  HIGH
    Test:   Paid an invoice in full, then reversed the payment from the Jurnal menu.
    Result: The money left the bank in the journal, but the invoice is still paid with
            residual 0. The receivable is understated, and the invoice will not chase
            payment.
    Why:    Invoices and bills are protected from reversal in this menu. Payment journals
            are not, and the software has no proper "cancel payment" action.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/accounting/accounting.module.ts:66

A4. Pricelist price is lost when quantity changes on the form  [UI]  HIGH
    Test:   PT Graha Bangun Sentosa has 5% off NYY cable from quantity 100. Picked the
            product, then typed quantity 150.
    Result: Discount stayed 0% and the price stayed 465.000. That is Rp 3.487.500 too much
            before tax, with no warning.
    Why:    The price is looked up once, when the product is picked at quantity 1. Changing
            the quantity does not look it up again. From the code, changing the customer
            afterwards has the same problem.
    Note:   demo-flow does not catch this. It sends no price, so the server fills it in.
    Where:  apps/web/src/pages/Sales.tsx:83, :144 and :166

A5. Principal credit note cannot reach "lunas" without overpaying  [TESTED]  HIGH
    Test:   Bill 3.263.400.000, credit note 1.110.000 including tax. Paid the displayed
            remaining debt of 3.262.290.000.
    Result: Status stays "sebagian" with remaining debt 0, and the bill itself still shows
            1.110.000 unpaid. Paying that 1.110.000 was accepted and reached "lunas", which
            is an overpayment. The PO page hides Bayar Principal at this point, but the
            bill page still offers Catat Pembayaran.
    Why:    The credit note lowers the displayed debt but is never applied to the bill.
    Where:  apps/api/src/common/status.ts:24, apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:209

A6. Editing a reopened order crashes if it already had a PO  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Test:   Confirm SO, create PO, cancel SO, Kembalikan ke Draf, change a line.
    Result: Error 500 "Internal server error". The log shows a foreign key violation. The
            user gets no explanation.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/sales/sales.service.ts:146

A7. Read-only users can send quotations  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Test:   Pimpinan and Operasional both have read-only access to Penjualan. Both sent a
            quotation.
    Result: An email goes to the customer and the status changes to Terkirim. Finance has
            the same read-only access, so it should behave the same (not run).
    Why:    The send endpoint only requires read permission.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/sales/sales.module.ts:24

A8. Invoices paid from an advance cannot be cancelled  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Test:   New customer, invoice fully covered by the advance, clicked the reversal.
    Result: Rejected with "Dokumen sudah dibayar sebagian". The invoice is fully paid, not
            partly paid. New customers must pay up front, so this applies to all of their
            invoices. The button is still shown.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:118

A9. PPN rounding leaves Rp 1 stuck on split deliveries  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Test:   SO total 3.330.133 delivered in 3 parts. Three invoices of 1.110.044 total
            3.330.132.
    Result: The SO shows Selesai, but Rp 1 stays in Uang Muka Pelanggan. Only a manual
            journal could clear it. PPN is rounded per document, so this can happen on any
            split delivery.
    Where:  apps/api/src/common/settings.service.ts (tax), billing.service.ts (invoiceFromSale)

A10. Product with no principal price gives a Rp 0 PO and 100% margin  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Test:   Product with no entry in the principal pricelist.
    Result: Cost 0, margin 100%, and a PO worth Rp 0 was created with no warning. An
            expired price would behave the same way (from the code).
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/master/pricing.service.ts:38

A11. PO PDF contradicts itself on PPN  [TESTED]  MEDIUM
    Result: The note says "Harga sudah termasuk PPN", but the same PDF adds PPN on top of
            the subtotal. A principal could read this either way, and a lecturer could
            notice it.
    Where:  apps/api/src/common/settings.service.ts:14 (the default text, also editable in
            Pengaturan > Dokumen)


3. B - BUSINESS RULES THAT NEED A DECISION
------------------------------------------

B1. When does a customer stop being "new"?  [TESTED]
    After a first order was delivered and fully paid, the customer was still marked new.
    The next order was blocked the same way. Only Pimpinan can turn it off by hand, and
    Admin cannot set it when creating a customer, so every customer Admin creates starts
    as new. Invoices for new customers are due the same day, even if their term of
    payment says otherwise.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/master/master.module.ts:13 and :46, billing.service.ts:54
    Decide: Switch off automatically after the first paid order, or stay manual? If manual,
            does Pimpinan get a reminder?

B2. What happens when a principal never ships the rest?  [TESTED]
    Received 198 of 397, then cancelled the follow-up receipt -T2.
      - The PO stays open at "menunggu barang" permanently.
      - The PO cannot be cancelled, because some goods were received.
      - The software still counts 199 as "on order", so no new PO is allowed for them.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/sales/sales.service.ts:206, purchasing.module.ts:129
    Decide: Add a "close PO short" action? Only un-received lines should stop counting.

B3. What happens to the money and PO when an SO is cancelled?  [TESTED]
    Cancelled an SO that had a confirmed PO and a Rp 5.000.000 advance.
      - The cancellation succeeds. The dialog mentions neither the money nor the PO.
      - The PO and its receipt stay active, and the goods can still arrive.
      - The advance stays on the cancelled SO. There is no refund or transfer function.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/sales/sales.service.ts:181
    Decide: Block the cancel, warn, or add refund and move-to-another-order functions?

B4. How are returns and stock corrections handled?  [CODE]
    I found no customer return, no return to principal, and no stock adjustment or stock
    count. A validated delivery cannot be cancelled. A wrong quantity can't be corrected
    in the software once validated. The invoice note says goods can't be returned, while
    the PO note says wrong goods go back to the seller.
    Decide: Out of scope for the prototype, or needed? If out of scope, say so in the book.

B5. Can MTPP pay a principal before the goods arrive?  [CODE]
    The seed data for PT Daya Pompa says the principal wants an advance payment and
    settlement before shipping. The software can't do this: a payment needs a posted bill,
    and a bill needs received goods.
    Where:  apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:140, apps/api/src/db/seed.ts:87
    Decide: Add principal advance payments, or change the seed note?

B6. Smaller rules to confirm
    a. Expired quotation [TESTED]: it shows as expired, but Konfirmasi still works. Allowed
       on purpose, or blocked?
    b. Siap Kirim [CODE]: Validasi is available directly from Draf, so this step can be
       skipped. demo-flow skips it too. Required or optional?
    c. New customer PO [CODE]: Finance can order from the principal before the customer
       pays anything, including dropship. Is that the intended risk?
    d. Regular customer advance [CODE]: the Proforma and Catat Uang Muka buttons only show
       for new customers. Can a regular customer never pay a down payment?
    e. Delivery warehouse [CODE]: a delivery can be switched to another principal's
       warehouse, but the reservation stays on the original warehouse, so stock figures
       can drift. Is moving between principals' warehouses allowed?
       (apps/api/src/modules/stock/transfers.service.ts:141)


4. C - CONFUSING SCREENS AND WORDING
------------------------------------

C1. Nothing tells the next person it is their turn  [CODE]
    Finance has no list of SOs that need a PO. Operasional has no list of SOs ready for a
    delivery order, because "Surat jalan belum dikirim" only counts delivery orders that
    already exist. Neither gets a notification. They must open each SO to find the button.
    Only invoicing has a worklist (Siap Ditagih).
    Suggestion: add "Perlu Pesanan Principal" and "Siap Dikirim" lists.

C2. Buttons that lead to an error or the wrong impression
    a. Dropship-only SO [TESTED]: Buat Surat Jalan is enabled, then fails with "Tidak ada
       sisa barang yang perlu dikirim". The real next step is the PO.
    b. Reversed invoice [CODE]: the status bar shows Lunas, though nothing was paid.
       (apps/web/src/pages/Billing.tsx:85)
    c. Reversal button [TESTED]: shown on invoices where it will always be rejected (A8).
    d. Send invoice email [CODE]: shown to anyone who can open the invoice, but only users
       with write access succeed.
    e. Invoice payment tab [CODE]: shows the full SO advance on every invoice. With 3
       invoices, each one lists the same full advance amount.
       (apps/api/src/modules/billing/billing.service.ts:40)

C3. Names that don't match
    a. A quotation is numbered SO/... before it is a sales order.
    b. Receipts are PO/...-T1, and follow-up receipts -T2. Dropship and follow-up deliveries
       get a new DO number, so a follow-up doesn't visibly link to its first delivery.
    c. Catat Uang Muka is also how a full settlement is recorded.
    d. The same action has several names: Posting / Konfirmasi tagihan, and Batalkan
       (pembalik) / jurnal pembalik / nota kredit.
    e. PO menu says Pesanan Principal; the PDF says Pesanan Pembelian.
    f. sequenceCode 'RCV' is still stored on the Penerimaan Barang type. It is not on
       screen and creates no number, so this only matters if someone reads the database.

C4. The same data in two menus
    Pelanggan, Principal and Pricelist each appear in both their module and Master. It
    isn't clear which one to use. They are the same data.

C5. README and software disagree
    The README lists reports under "Seluruh peran", but Admin has 0 reports and the Laporan
    menu is hidden, even though Admin has read permission for it. The other groups have:
    Pimpinan 12, Finance 7, Sales 5, Operasional 3 [TESTED].
    Where: apps/api/src/common/menus.ts:44


5. BEFORE ANY REAL DEPLOYMENT  [CODE]
-------------------------------------
Fine for a local demo, but these must change before real use:
  - The login page shows the demo accounts and password (apps/web/src/pages/Home.tsx:26).
  - JWT_SECRET has a known default value if none is set (docker-compose.yml:24).
  - The login token is put in the image URL for signatures (apps/web/src/pages/Home.tsx:113).
    URLs can end up in logs and browser history.
  - Without a Brevo key, emails show as "terkirim" even though nothing was sent. The README
    documents this, but it can mislead during a demo.
